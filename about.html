About Oral Lesion

Learn more about our mission to detect oral cancer early and save lives.

Our Story

OralVision was born out of a research project at GM University's Department of Medical Imaging and Artificial Intelligence. Our team of medical professionals and AI specialists recognized the critical need for accessible, early-stage oral cancer detection.

Oral cancer is often diagnosed in late stages, significantly reducing survival rates. However, when detected early, the 5-year survival rate can be as high as 90%. This stark contrast motivated us to develop an AI-powered solution that could make screening more accessible to everyone.

Since our inception, we've collaborated with leading oncologists, dentists, and AI researchers to train our models on thousands of medical images, resulting in a highly accurate detection system that continues to improve with each scan.

Our Mission

To reduce oral cancer mortality rates by making early detection accessible to everyone.

Our Vision

A world where no one dies from oral cancer due to late diagnosis.
