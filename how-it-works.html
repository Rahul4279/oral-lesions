How OralVision Works

Our AI-powered platform makes it easy to check for oral health concerns in three simple steps.

Upload Your Image

Take a clear photo of the concerned area inside your mouth and upload it to our secure platform.

AI Analysis

Our advanced AI technology analyzes your image to detect potential signs of oral lesions or cancer.

Get Results

Receive a detailed assessment with clear next steps and recommendations for follow-up care if needed.

Our Technology

OralVision utilizes state-of-the-art deep learning algorithms developed by researchers at GM University to analyze oral cavity images with extraordinary precision.

The AI has been trained on thousands of verified medical images, enabling it to identify subtle patterns and characteristics associated with various oral conditions, including pre-cancerous lesions.

  • Deep learning neural networks
  • Trained on extensive medical datasets
  • End-to-end encryption for data privacy
  • Regular updates to improve accuracy
